# Technical specifications: The Pulse of the Earth

Supplements the general `technical-specifications.md`: technical choices specific to this visualization, beyond the common rules.

**Status: implemented.**

## Data source

Eruption catalog of the Global Volcanism Program (Smithsonian), see "Source dataset", "License" and "Data retrieval" in `data-model.md`. One-off retrieval at preprocessing time (the GVP's WFS web service, no HTML scraping), not at every build. One-off Node preprocessing script, not committed (same convention as all previous visualizations, see for example "Preprocessing" in `specs/bird-migrations/data-model.md`), to be rerun manually if the GVP catalog is updated.

## Map technology

Same stack as `bird-migrations`/`monarch-migration`/`earthquakes`, D3 v7, no Leaflet.

- **Basemap, rivers, relief: copied (identical bytes) from `public/data/bird-migrations/`** (`basemap.json`, `rivers.json`, `relief.webp`) to `public/data/volcanic-eruptions/`, rather than referenced directly: copying rather than cross-folder referencing, a convention confirmed by `monarch-migration` and `earthquakes` (see their respective `technical-specifications.md`). No tectonic plate boundaries here (specific to `earthquakes`, out of scope for this visualization).
- The relief alignment constants (`RELIEF_REF_WIDTH/HEIGHT`, `RELIEF_ORIGIN_X/Y`) are a physical property of the `relief.webp` file itself: reused identically from `bird-migrations`/`earthquakes`, independently of this visualization's own `fitExtent` framing (whole world map, like `earthquakes`).
- **Projection:** `d3.geoNaturalEarth1`, `fitExtent` on the full extent of the silhouette (whole world map visible by default).

## Rendering

**Canvas 2D**, same choice as `bird-migrations`/`earthquakes`/`satellites-in-orbit`/`monument-layers`: 10,937 eruptions (11,089 in the raw Holocene catalog, 111 earlier than -8000 excluded, 41 outside the Holocene volcano catalog excluded, see "Eruptions outside the Holocene volcano catalog" in `data-model.md`) and 1,214 volcanoes (see "Data retrieval" and "Source fields used" in `data-model.md`), the same order of magnitude as `earthquakes` (14,453 earthquakes).

- Two stacked canvases: a background canvas (silhouette, rivers, relief, redrawn only on resize/zoom) and a foreground canvas (resting volcano points + pulses), same architecture as `bird-migrations`/`earthquakes`.
- **Volcanoes at rest:** a discreet, permanent point (fixed radius 2.5 px, neutral tint `#6b6455`, see "Palette" below and its entry in `style-guide.md` "Basemap (illustration)") marks the position of each volcano at all times, including the 333 volcanoes in the catalog with no dated eruption (see "Source fields used" in `data-model.md`). Drawn on the background canvas (redrawn only on resize/zoom, not at every animation frame) rather than on the foreground canvas: unlike the pulses, their position never changes between two frames, and redrawing them continuously there would be a needless cost (1,214 points at every frame, versus a few dozen active pulses at most). Not clipped to the Earth silhouette (like the tectonic plate boundaries of `earthquakes`): some volcanoes are in coastal areas where the silhouette outline may diverge slightly from the exact position of the point.
- **Eruption pulse:** at the simulated instant an eruption occurs, a circle is drawn at the volcano's point, with radius and initial opacity increasing with the eruption's VEI (`d3.scaleSqrt`, domain `[0, 8]`, undetermined VEI treated as 0, see "Undetermined VEI" in `data-model.md`), then expands and fades over a short fixed duration (700 ms of real time, independent of the chosen playback speed: a pulse must remain visible and readable even at "Fast" speed).
- **Permanent detail card:** unlike `earthquakes` (whose hit-test only covers active pulses), hover/tap covers the 1,214 permanent points at all times (see "Volcano hover/tap" in `functional-specifications.md`): search for the permanent point closest to the cursor/tap, not only the ongoing pulses.
- **Text annotation** (notable eruptions, see "Selection of eruptions to annotate" in `data-model.md`): DOM element overlaid on the Canvas (same approach as `earthquakes`), positioned near the point of the volcano concerned, appearing in sync with the pulse, lingering 2,200 ms (same duration as `earthquakes`) to remain readable after the pulse fades out.

## Palette

**Sequential palette on the VEI**, with a hue specific to this visualization (orange, `H≈41°` in OKLCH), not the default blue palette of `style-guide.md` (reserved by `earthquakes` for magnitude): visually distinguishes the two most similar visualizations on the site (same basemap, same continuous-loop pulse mechanism). Generated by the same method as the blue ramp (same OKLCH lightness steps, hue rotated toward the categorical orange `#eb6834`), validated with the dataviz skill's validator (`validate_palette.js`, lightness monotonicity and single hue confirmed; the light-end contrast floor, specific to the "Ordinal" job, does not apply here: "Sequential" job, same status as the blue ramp already in production on `earthquakes`, see the skill's `references/color-formula.md`).

- VEI domain `[0, 8]` (official GVP scale; VEI 7 is the maximum actually observed in the Holocene catalog, no VEI 8 documented).
- `VOLCANIC_SEQUENTIAL_ORANGE: [string, string] = ['#ffd3c2', '#631b00']` (lightest → darkest step). Low VEI: light, discreet pulse. High VEI: wide, intense pulse (`#631b00`, the most saturated).
- Resting volcano point (see "Rendering" above): `#6b6455`, fixed neutral tint, outside this scale, added to `style-guide.md` ("Basemap (illustration)").

## Animation

- `d3-timer` loop, driven by a continuous simulated year over the domain `[-8000, 2026]` (actual bounds of the catalog after excluding pre-Holocene eruptions, see "Source fields used" in `data-model.md`), i.e. about 10,026 years, **looped continuously** (see "Looping autoplay" in `functional-specifications.md`) rather than a single pass, with no final pause (same convention as `bird-migrations`/`earthquakes`).
- Since `eruptions` is sorted by ascending `year` (see "Validation constraints" in `data-model.md`), an index cursor walks the list at each frame to trigger the pulses whose simulated year has just been reached. Same fine spreading of eruptions of the same year over `[year, year+1)` as `earthquakes` (`assignSimYears`), to smooth the triggering rate rather than a simultaneous burst at each whole-year change.
- Speed selector: changes the total duration of a full cycle (factors ×0.5/×1/×2, identical to `bird-migrations`/`earthquakes`), not the display duration of an individual pulse (see "Rendering" above, pulse duration fixed in real time).
- **Total cycle duration at "Normal" speed: 120 seconds**, calibrated by simulation on the actual catalog (actual peak of 54 eruptions in a single calendar year, 2004) then confirmed by direct observation in the browser (see "Points decided during implementation" below), longer than the 90 seconds of `earthquakes`, consistent with an eruption volume 23% lower (11,089 versus 14,453) but spread over a period 80 times longer and the unprecedented order of magnitude of time compression of the subject (ten thousand years).
- **Simulated year display:** explicit sign rather than `Intl.DateTimeFormat` (limited to year 1 and not designed for very old dates): `Intl.NumberFormat(lang).format(Math.abs(Math.floor(simYear)))` followed by the `yearBcSuffix` suffix ("av. J.-C." / "BC") if the year is negative, with no suffix otherwise (same convention as `centuryBcSuffix` in `monument-layers`, no explicit "apr. J.-C." suffix for positive years, consistent with common usage).

## New dependencies

No new dependency compared to `bird-migrations`/`earthquakes`: `d3-geo`, `d3-scale`, `d3-timer`, `d3-zoom`, `d3-selection`, `topojson-client` are enough (already present in `package.json`).

## Hydration

Mount area hydrated via a classic `<script>` setting up an `IntersectionObserver` (`rootMargin: 200px`) that only mounts the rendering on the first entry into the viewport, same mechanism as `earthquakes`/`bird-migrations` (no Astro `client:visible` directive strictly speaking: these components use no UI framework, see "Hydration" in the general `technical-specifications.md`).

## Accessibility (known limitation)

See "Accessibility (known limitation)" in this visualization's `functional-specifications.md`. Play/Pause button as a standard `<button>` with synchronized `aria-pressed`, year slider as a native `<input type="range">`, speed selector as a native `<select>` with `aria-label`: all three keyboard accessible by construction, same pattern as `earthquakes`.

## Responsive

See "Responsive" in this visualization's `functional-specifications.md`. Touch pan/zoom via `d3-zoom`, tooltip triggered by `pointerdown`/`click` rather than `pointermove` on touch, zoom in/zoom out/reset buttons in addition (top right of the map area), same mechanism as `bird-migrations`/`monarch-migration`/`earthquakes`.

## State in the URL

See "State in the URL" in the general `technical-specifications.md` for the shared mechanism.

| Parameter | Values | Default (absent from the URL) |
|---|---|---|
| `year` | Integer year within the covered period (-8000 to the last year of the catalog), negative before the Common Era | Playback running |
| `zoom` | `<k>,<lon>,<lat>` (shared format) | Whole world map |

- **Departure from the general temporal position rule, same treatment as `earthquakes`:** once playback stops, pulses fade within 700 ms and the annotation within 2,200 ms (see "Rendering" above), and the resting volcano points are the same whatever the year. A paused image on a shared year would therefore show nothing specific to that year, apart from the indicator. A link carrying `year` therefore restarts playback from that year instead of opening it paused. The URL then removes `year` as soon as it mounts, since playback is running: this is the only URL write that does not follow a visitor action.
- **Exception, reduced motion** (see "Reduced motion" in the general `functional-specifications.md`): `year` is not removed on mount and the visualization stays paused on the shared year, the visitor's preference taking precedence over restarting playback. Without `year`, it opens paused at the start of the period.
- `year` is written on pause (button) and on release of the year slider, and removed when playback resumes.

## Points decided during implementation

- **GVP license verified** (see "License" in `data-model.md`): non-commercial use with citation, WFS web service accessible without anti-bot blocking (unlike the main HTML site).
- **Actual volume confirmed**: 1,214 volcanoes, 11,089 Holocene eruptions (including 915 volcanoes with at least one dated eruption), readable in Canvas without overload (same order of magnitude as `earthquakes`).
- **Selection criterion for notable eruptions:** VEI ≥ 6 (54 eruptions within the domain `[-8000, 2026]`) + Vesuvius 79 (the only candidate anticipated during scoping below this threshold), i.e. 55 notable eruptions in total, see "Selection of eruptions to annotate" in `data-model.md`.
- **"Normal" cycle duration:** 120 seconds (see "Animation" above).
- **Reuse of the `basemap.json`/`rivers.json`/`relief.webp` files:** byte-for-byte copy from `bird-migrations`, like `monarch-migration`/`earthquakes` (see "Map technology" above).
